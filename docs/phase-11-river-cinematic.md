# Phase 11 — River Traversal & Cinematic Dialogue

## Objective

Add the Great Usuthu River as a traversable zone and implement a cinematic dialogue camera system. Both systems draw from the same cultural source: the Usuthu is Eswatini's sacred river and a historical trade corridor, not set dressing.

---

## 1. River Traversal System

### 1A — Great Usuthu River Waterway

**Data source**: Copernicus DEM river channel + OSM waterway polygon for the Usuthu from Mhlambanyatsi to Lavumisa. Already in `pipeline/terrain/data/` as extracted river network from DEM hydrology pass.

**New Actor**: `Source/MahlanyaRPG/Environment/WaterTraversal/AUsuthuRiverZone.h`
- River zone trigger volume (UBoxComponent) — detects when player enters navigable water
- `GetCurrentStrength(FVector Location) → float` — current speed in m/s from hydrology data; reads precomputed `data/usuthu_flow.json` (generated by `pipeline/terrain/river_extraction.py`)
- `IsNavigable(FVector Location) → bool` — depth > 0.8 m AND width > 2 m at query point

**Canoe Actor**: `Source/MahlanyaRPG/Vehicles/AUsuthuCanoe.h`
- Static mesh: `Content/Vehicles/Canoe/SM_UsuthuCanoe.uasset` (Blender pipeline — see `pipeline/art/blender-pipeline.md`)
- Physics: buoyancy via UE5 `UBuoyancyComponent` on 4 pontoon points
- Paddle animation: `ABP_CanoePlayer` blends left/right paddle strokes based on `InputAction_Paddle_Left` / `InputAction_Paddle_Right`
- Current drag: `AUsuthuCanoe::TickComponent` adds `CurrentStrength × FlowDirection` force each tick
- Boarding: interact with canoe on bank → player character mounts, camera switches to `CameraMode_Canoe`

**Historical constraint**: Eswatini canoes (pre-1902) are dugout mokoros, not plank-built. Mesh must reflect single-log hull. CI historical accuracy check covers this — `scripts/historical_accuracy_check.py` validates mesh metadata tag `hull_type: dugout_mokoro`.

### 1B — Canoe Camera Mode

`Source/MahlanyaRPG/Camera/UCanoeCameraMode.h` — extends `UMahlanyaCameraMode`
- Spring arm: distance 350 cm, pitch −15°, yaw follows canoe heading with 3s lag
- On rapid water (flow > 2.5 m/s): FOV pushes from 80° to 90° over 0.5s via timeline
- Dismount: smooth blend back to third-person mode over 0.8s

## 2. Cinematic Dialogue Camera System

Reference: Black Myth: Wukong opening sequence — camera cuts between character close-ups during dialogue, orbits slowly, returns to gameplay camera on dialogue end. Subtitle placement at bottom-centre, large readable font.

### 2A — Cinematic Camera Director

`Source/MahlanyaRPG/Cinematics/ACinematicDialogueDirector.h`

Shot types (enum `ECinematicShotType`):
- `CLOSE_UP` — face at 50–70 cm, slight low angle (+5°)
- `MEDIUM_TWO_SHOT` — both speakers visible, camera at chest height
- `OVER_SHOULDER` — speaker A behind, speaker B in frame
- `SLOW_ORBIT` — 360° arc at 2°/s around primary speaker

Camera selection logic:
1. Line of dialogue starts → Director picks shot type from `UDialogueAsset::ShotHints[]`
2. Cut to chosen camera with 0.15s blend (fast cut, not cross-fade — matches reference style)
3. Slow orbit fires on monologues > 4 lines
4. Dialogue ends → return to gameplay camera, 0.8s blend

`AUsuthuDialogueActor` — NPC class with `TArray<FDialogueLine> Lines` and `UDialogueAsset* Asset` — triggers Director on interact.

### 2B — Subtitle Renderer

`Source/MahlanyaRPG/UI/USubtitleWidget.h` — UMG widget, `WBP_CinematicSubtitle.uasset`
- Font: `MahlanyaSerif_SiSwati` (existing font asset, used in quest UI)
- Layout: bottom-centre, 80% screen width, max 2 lines, 24sp base (scales with UI scale curve)
- Both siSwati and English subtitles rendered simultaneously — siSwati above English in smaller text
- Fade in: 100ms / Fade out: 200ms on line change
- `bSubtitlesEnabled` exposed to accessibility settings (already in `Config/DefaultGame.ini`)

### 2C — Cinematic Sequence — Usuthu Crossing

`Content/Cinematics/CS_UsuthuCrossing.uasset` — Level Sequence

Story beat: Elder Ntombi warns Mahlanya about the ukuphiwa (gift/tribute) required to cross the Usuthu — a spiritual toll, not a material one. This is historically grounded in Swazi river crossing traditions.

Beat structure:
1. MEDIUM_TWO_SHOT — Mahlanya approaches bank, Ntombi seated
2. CLOSE_UP Ntombi — "Lomfula kawulelwa yonke umuntu" (This river does not open for everyone)
3. OVER_SHOULDER Mahlanya — player input: choose response (3 options)
4. SLOW_ORBIT — Ntombi explains the ukuphiwa, camera orbits her
5. Cut to canoe on bank — gameplay resumes

Cultural review required before merge per `docs/cultural-review-protocol.md`. Reviewer must not be the author.
