## 5. Historical Content Notice

The Game depicts historical events, persons, places, and cultural practices of the Kingdom of Eswatini (Swaziland) spanning the period approximately 1750 to 1906. All historical persons, places, and events are portrayed with documentary accuracy based on available historical record, including primary ethnographic sources, colonial-era records, and contemporary Swazi historiography. Dramatized dialogue and speculative fills are clearly distinguished in the in-game Chronicle system. The Developer does not intend these depictions to be taken as authoritative scholarly history; users seeking academic sources should consult the bibliography available at the Developer's website.
