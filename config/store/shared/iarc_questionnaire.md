# IARC Content Rating Questionnaire — Mahlanya RPG

**Game:** Mahlanya RPG
**Developer:** BRT Inc. / Charles Bartaria
**Date:** 2026-07-01
**Platforms:** PC (Steam), iOS, Android

---

## Violence

**Q: Does the game include violence?**
Yes.

**Q: What type of violence?**
Player-controlled combat using traditional Swazi weapons: the Umshiza (fighting stick) and spear. Cattle raid sequences depict armed conflict between NPC groups and the player. Historical battle sequences (Battle of Lubombo, 1846) depict organised military engagement with period-accurate weapons.

**Q: How severe is the violence?**
Moderate. Characters are struck and defeated in combat. No blood pools, gore, or dismemberment are depicted. Defeated characters fall and become incapacitated; no death animations beyond collapse.

**Q: Is violence player-controlled?**
Yes — combat is initiated and executed by the player. Some scripted historical battle sequences are partially player-controlled within a guided scenario.

**Q: Does violence target human characters?**
Yes — NPCs representing historical Swazi, Zulu, and colonial-era figures. No targeting of real named historical individuals in combat.

**Q: Is there cartoon or fantasy violence?**
No. The visual style is realistic (Unreal Engine 5). Violence is depicted as consequential, not stylised or comedic.

**Q: Is there violence against animals?**
Cattle are the economic core of the game but are not harmed in graphic detail. Raid sequences may depict cattle being driven off; no animal cruelty mechanics.

**Q: Does the game depict colonial violence?**
Yes — the colonial resistance era (1880–1906) depicts the political and physical consequences of colonial subjugation, including land alienation, armed encounters with colonial forces, and the erosion of Swazi sovereignty. This is depicted from the Swazi perspective with historical accuracy. It is presented as mature historical content, not gratuitous.

**Resulting violence classification:** Moderate Violence / Mature Historical Themes

## Language

**Q: Does the game contain profanity or strong language?**
No. The game is voiced in siSwati with English subtitles. No profanity in English. siSwati dialogue follows cultural norms of the depicted historical period; no offensive or derogatory language is present.

**Q: Does the game contain slurs or hate speech?**
No. Historical colonial-era terminology that would be considered slurs is not used. Dialogue accurately represents historical social structures without deploying dehumanising language.

**Resulting language classification:** None

---

## Sexual Content

**Q: Does the game contain nudity?**
No. The Umhlanga (Reed Dance) ceremony is depicted in accordance with historical documentation; ceremonial dress is rendered respectfully and does not constitute nudity for rating purposes.

**Q: Does the game contain sexual content or acts?**
No.

**Q: Does the game contain suggestive or mature sexual themes?**
No. Lobola (cattle bride price) is implemented as a cultural and economic mechanic — a transaction of cattle establishing kinship alliance. No romantic or sexual content accompanies it.

**Resulting sexual content classification:** None

## Controlled Substances

**Q: Does the game depict alcohol, tobacco, or drugs?**
No. Traditional Swazi ceremonies depicted in the game do not include the use of substances in any interactive or depicted form.

**Q: Does the game reference or glamorise drug use?**
No.

**Resulting controlled substances classification:** None

## Horror / Fear

**Q: Does the game contain horror elements or content designed to frighten?**
No. The game does not contain jump scares, supernatural horror, psychological horror mechanics, or content designed to provoke fear as an entertainment mechanism.

**Q: Does the game depict disturbing themes?**
Yes — mature historical themes. The colonial resistance era depicts political subjugation, displacement, and the destruction of the Swazi political economy. These are presented as serious historical content, not as horror entertainment. The emotional weight is intentional and historically grounded.

**Resulting horror/fear classification:** Not applicable as horror; classified under Mature Historical Themes

---

## Social Features

**Q: Does the game include online multiplayer?**
Yes — co-op multiplayer for 2–4 players in cattle raid and diplomatic escort missions.

**Q: Does the game include voice chat?**
No. Voice chat is not implemented. Text communication is available in siSwati and English only.

**Q: Does the game include in-game messaging or social features?**
No persistent messaging system. In-session co-op text chat only, limited to mission-relevant communication.

**Q: Can players interact with strangers online?**
Yes — co-op matchmaking may pair players who do not know each other. No persistent social graph or friend-list system is required.

**Resulting social features classification:** Users Interact (limited; co-op sessions only, no persistent social network)

## User-Generated Content

**Q: Does the game allow users to create and share content?**
No. There is no UGC system. Players cannot create mods, levels, characters, or any content shareable within the game platform.

**Q: Does the game include user-uploaded images, audio, or video?**
No.

**Resulting UGC classification:** None

## Gambling

**Q: Does the game include gambling mechanics?**
No. The cattle economy uses in-game cattle as currency only. No real-money wagering, no simulated gambling mechanics (no card games, dice wagering, loot boxes, or chance-based purchase systems).

**Q: Are there loot boxes or random reward purchases?**
No. The game is a single paid purchase. No in-app purchases of any kind.

**Resulting gambling classification:** None
